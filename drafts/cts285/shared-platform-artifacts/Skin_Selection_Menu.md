## Reading the menu

**The vibe column is a design brief in miniature.** "Cozy, handmade, sensory" and "Sleek, cutting-edge" are the same Commerce Tracking Engine and they are not the same product — different typography, different photography, different words for the same button. That difference is what your design partner works on in Week 15, and it is why the skin is a real decision rather than a coat of paint.

**Two skins on the same engine are still different products.** PawHealth and MindCalm both log values over time against goals. One needs a species field and a vet-visit history; the other needs a tone that does not gamify a bad week. Neither requirement comes from the engine.

**Proposing your own is allowed** and is not penalized. It has to run on one of the four engines, and you have to be able to say which — the same selection evidence the Week 13 assignment asks for either way.

**On the satirical four.** They are products on this menu, not a joke sitting beside it. The bar is the same: a coherent identity, a plausible market, and a build that works. If the satire is the only thing holding the product together, the product is not holding together.
