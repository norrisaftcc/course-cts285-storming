🎭 Sprint 4 Theme: Design Integration & Collaboration Close-Out

Why this sprint matters:

This is the final sprint where you work with GRD-242 design students. After this module, you are on your own. Everything you need from the design team must be finalized, integrated, and documented now. There are no do-overs.

Key Responsibilities This Sprint:

  • Evaluate every design deliverable against your acceptance criteria — does it meet the spec?
  • Integrate final design assets (logos, color schemes, layouts, icons) into your codebase
  • Run acceptance tests to confirm design assets render correctly in your application
  • Document all design attributions and licensing
  • Close the GRD-242 collaboration professionally — thank your design partners
  • Reflect on what worked and what you would do differently in cross-team collaboration

Professional Reality: In industry, handoffs between teams happen constantly. Learning to evaluate deliverables, provide structured feedback, and close collaborations gracefully is a career-defining skill.

💡 Andrew's Tips for Success

  • Don't accept "close enough" on design assets: If an asset doesn't meet your acceptance criteria, send it back with specific, actionable feedback. Being polite but firm is professional.
  • Test assets in context, not in isolation: A logo that looks great in Figma might break your layout. Always test in your actual application.
  • Document attribution NOW: You'll need this for your final portfolio. Don't try to reconstruct who made what in Week 16.
  • Close the collaboration gracefully: Send a thank-you message to your design partners. In industry, your reputation follows you between teams.
  • The midcourse self-assessment is for YOU: Be brutally honest. The goals you set here will guide the solo development phase. Generic goals produce generic results.
  • Take before/after screenshots: Documenting the transformation from placeholder to final design is powerful portfolio material.

📣 Andrew's Note

This module marks a turning point in your capstone. After Sprint 4, the GRD-242 collaboration ends and you enter solo development mode. Everything you need from the design team must be locked down this week.

I've seen students lose days in Modules 6-7 because they realized too late that a design asset was missing or wrong. Do not let that be you. Use your evaluation framework. Check every asset. Get sign-off in writing.

The midcourse self-assessment is also a big deal. You've been through CSC-113, CSC-114, and now half of CSC-289. You've grown more than you realize. Take the time to reflect on that growth — and be specific about where you want to go from here.

Close this collaboration like a professional. The design students worked hard for you. Acknowledge it.
