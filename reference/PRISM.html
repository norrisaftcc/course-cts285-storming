PRISM

From First Commit to Field Leadership — a visible progression of what you can build, own, and lead at each stage of your journey, and what you bank into your Module Zero record along the way.

Warm — makers

What you can build. Governed by Bloom's: the cognitive complexity of the work rises with the color.

Green — the pivot

Job-ready. The apex of making and the hinge where building starts to scale into leading.

Cool — orchestrators

What you can coordinate. Past "create," the lens shifts from cognition to scope of ownership.

Module Zero + is the engine: each color names what a student banks into their living competency record. Because Module Zero recurs in every course, those gains accumulate across the whole certificate.

PRISM. Course placements are generic placeholders and can be aligned to specific CTI/CIT courses. The framework reframes progression as visible growth: each color represents evidence of increasing capability, ownership, and impact accumulated through authentic work. What begins as guided participation develops into independent delivery, leadership, and ultimately influence across projects, programs, and organizations.
