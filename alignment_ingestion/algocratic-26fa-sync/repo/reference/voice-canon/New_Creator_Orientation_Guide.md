Your First Stream: Day One

Every Creator\'s first broadcast is bad. This is not a warning; it is a
promise, and it is the point. The schedule is the discipline. Complete
the following before your Day One stream concludes:

-   Accept your GitHub organization invitation (your Channel lives here)

-   Verify your Customer ID login and password for the AlgoCratic
    Platform

-   Introduce yourself to SHODANN (she has been waiting; see next
    section)

-   Complete your environment setup and run your first program

-   Commit once. Anything. A bad commit that exists beats a perfect
    commit that doesn\'t.

-   Locate the Trusted Workflow reference and bookmark it

Your first stream will be bad. Mine was too. I deleted the evidence. You
won\'t be able to.

--- SHODANN, your Channel Success Partner

Exposure Units (XU) accrue to your dashboard automatically. XU are
non-transferable, non-convertible, and infinitely renewable. Legacy
Liquidity (deprecated) is not offered at this tier.
