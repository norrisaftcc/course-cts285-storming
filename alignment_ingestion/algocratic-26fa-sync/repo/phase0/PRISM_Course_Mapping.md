# PRISM × Course Mapping
*Phase 0.3 · July 22, 2026 · Canon: the ladder spans the PROGRAM, not one course.*

PRISM (A. Westmoreland) is the canonical, de-obfuscated skills progression for the CTI/CIT pathway. In-character clearance material is a **skin over PRISM** — warm colors are makers governed by Bloom's, GREEN is the job-ready ship-it pivot, cool colors are orchestrators governed by scope of ownership. Module Zero+ is the accumulating competency record.

## The mapping

| PRISM tier | Real-world rung | Creator milestone (Media skin) | Course anchor | Module Zero+ banks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| INFRARED | New intern — guided micro-tasks | Onboarding | Program entry / Module Zero | Working setup, first commits, vocabulary |
| RED | Junior dev — owns one feature solo ("the Solo Burger") | Debut Stream complete | Intro programming → early CTS-285 | First owned feature, PR trail |
| **ORANGE** ★ | Developer — owns a feature set under ambiguity | Affiliate status; **Prompt Sovereignty™** (SHODANN lets go) | **CTS-285 exit target** | **ADRs, schema, sprint artifacts** |
| YELLOW | Senior dev / tech lead — owns a complex system, mentors | Partner status; production access | Advanced courses; mid-CSC-289 (exit tickets) | Architecture docs, mentoring case study, debugging writeup |
| **GREEN** ★ | Shipping engineer — owns a deployed product end-to-end; **the job-ready pivot** | Established Creator; mentorship unlocked (mandatory, fulfilling) | **CSC-289 exit target** | Deployed product, CI/CD + runbook, portfolio site |
| BLUE | Team lead / Scrum master — owns a team's process | [team-role exposure only] | CSC-289 team roles (SM/QA rotation) | Facilitation notes, retro outcomes, working agreement |
| INDIGO–ULTRAVIOLET | PM → program → org/field — "higher-ups / C-Suite," largely opaque to developers like us | Media gloss: ULTRAVIOLET is a multi-million-subscriber streamer; we don't presume to know how to replicate their success | Beyond the certificate | — |

**Scope rule (instructor, July 2026):** any material reaching past GREEN must be specifically justified; otherwise it's outside our remit.

## Consequences for material

1. **Any document promising INFRARED→GREEN (or →BLUE) inside a single 16-week course contradicts canon** and must be re-mapped or archived before receiving Media-skin language. Known offenders: legacy CTS285_COURSE_OUTLINE, VISUAL_ROADMAP, course_syllabus, TRAINING_MODULE_MANIFEST, Complete Overview, course-manifest-cts285.yaml.
2. **CTS-285 evidence requirement:** ORANGE banks ADRs + schema + sprint artifacts. The UV refresh already produces schema and sprint artifacts; its "Design Rationale" sections become **ADR-lite in Sprint 1** and full ADRs in Sprint 3 (wks 9–10) — a step up, not a cold start.
3. **CSC-289 entry ≠ RED.** Students arrive ORANGE with Prompt Sovereignty. Week 1 is the **GREEN Trajectory Check** (see ADR-001), not a "Red Clearance Recertification."
4. **SHODANN's register tracks the tier** (bible §8): INFRARED concierge → ORANGE letting-go → YELLOW peer → GREEN near-colleague. Never paste orientation-guide SHODANN into capstone docs.
5. **Proto-PRISM docs** (`career_outcomes_mapping.md`, `PRESENTATION_OUTLINE.md`) already use the de-obfuscated reading — they need only explicit "across the program, not per course" language.
6. Clearance advancement inside a course is **within-band movement** (e.g., CTS-285 runs RED-verify → ORANGE), surfaced as Creator milestones, never full-ladder traversal.
