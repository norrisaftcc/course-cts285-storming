# Kayfabe Architecture: The Two Layers
*Phase 0 addendum · v0.1 (evolving — instructor owns; expect revision as the idea matures) · July 2026*

## The Ruling

AlgoCratic Media and AlgoCratic Futures are **not two skins. They are two layers of one kayfabe.**

**One company.** AlgoCratic Futures™ is the workplace — the satirical corporate dystopia where software gets built under The Algorithm. AlgoCratic Media is **Futures' own in-house content division** (the orientation guide's masthead already says so: "a division of AlgoCratic Futures™"). Creators are dev-influencers whose job is to *perform their software-development job in public.* The student plays a content creator; the content the creator makes is software development.

This is the build-in-public / dev-influencer satire, and like every good AlgoCratic joke it is literally true: learning in public genuinely is best practice, narrated work genuinely is the portfolio, and XU-as-Exposure genuinely is the thing employers look at.

Precedent: the character-playing-a-role tradition of content creation — a persona who is "a developer who can explain things" (AVGN reviewing games; VTubers streaming them). Explaining is the performance.

## The Layer Map

| Layer | Who/what | Vocabulary | Voice | Progression |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **L0 — Real** | The student, the instructor, the actual grade | Plain English | Instructor OOC ("Andrew's Note") | The transcript; Module Zero+ competency record |
| **L1 — Platform (AlgoCratic Media)** | The Creator and their Channel; SHODANN; XU | Creator, Channel, Content, stream, episode | SHODANN (first-person, warm-deadpan, delta-only) | Creator milestones: Debut Stream → Affiliate → Partner → Established |
| **L2 — The Show (AlgoCratic Futures)** | The developer character working under The Algorithm | Clearance, directives, ™-policy, the Trapezoid | Corporate (cheerfully contradictory) | PRISM clearance: INFRARED → … → GREEN |

The two progressions mirror each other (the bible's tier table already runs both columns): clearance is what the *character* earns in the show; milestones are what the *Creator* earns on the platform. XU accrues at L1 for work performed at L2.

**Failure physics (the pedagogical core):** failure at L2 is *content* at L1. "My character's build failed, and my Creator got a good episode out of it" puts two layers between the student and the fear point. Struggle that is real exits to L0 — SHODANN hands off to the human, always (bible §9).

**Voice sort:** Corporate speaks at L2. SHODANN speaks at L1 (which is *why* she never uses doublespeak — she's platform-side, not show-side). Instructor speaks at L0. The Underground lives between layers — see Off-Camera below.

## Persona Policy (lightweight, opt-in deeper)

Week 1 requires exactly one persona act: **name your channel.** By default the Creator is the student's professional self. Students who want a named developer-character distinct from themselves may build one — materials support this and never require it. No persona-creation exercise may eat day-1 time, and no rubric may score persona depth.

Guardrail: the persona is armor, not an exit. "It's just my character" never excuses the work — XU accrues only to real commits, real reviews, real shipped things.

## Mechanics Unlocked (baked in)

1. **Off-Camera.** The Underground formalized: what happens *off-stream*. Private drafts, scratch branches, struggle space — the persona drops, no XU is at stake, SHODANN does not see it (canonically; see bible §1 on the margins). This gives the existing Underground voice a structural home in the new model: the resistance materials are what Creators pass around off-camera. `frotz → plugh` still opens the door.
2. **Episodes & seasons.** Sprints are episodes; the semester is a season; retrospectives are highlight reels. CTS-285 wks 11–12 (Launch Content Sprint + grouped Dataman presentations) is the **Season 1 finale**. CSC-289 is **Season 2**.
3. **Collab channels.** Wk-13 group formation is Creators forming a **collab channel** for a joint Season 2 production. The 285→289 handoff package is, in-world, the collab channel's season plan. Team contract = collab agreement.

**Deferred (noted, not baked):** the explainer-persona assessment hook — grading teach-backs and narrated demos explicitly as content quality. Revisit after the fall run shows how much narration students produce unprompted.

## Guardrails

- **Lore tax.** This document is authoring doctrine (GREY-side), not a cosmology students must learn. Students experience one coherent world; the layers are load-bearing for *writers*, visible to students only if they look. Never open a student-facing document by explaining the layer model. (House memo: "stop building encyclopedia.")
- The bible's never-list (§9) applies at every layer. The retired punitive register stays retired — L2's oppression is aimed at the fictional company, never at a Creator's standing.
- When layers conflict in a document, sort by voice: Corporate body (L2) + SHODANN interjections (L1) + instructor margins (L0) + fine print / margins (Off-Camera). That is the existing document architecture — this model explains it rather than changing it.

## Executor Instructions

When writing or converting material: decide which layer each passage speaks from before writing it; use the layer's vocabulary and voice only; let L2 stay absurd and L1 stay warm; route anything real to L0. If a joke requires a Creator to be threatened, it is at the wrong layer — move the threat to the show's policies and let SHODANN decline to endorse it.
